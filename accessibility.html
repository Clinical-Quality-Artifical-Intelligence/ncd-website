Accessibility

Last updated 20 November 2026

We want everyone to be able to use this website. It is built to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 at level AA. You should be able to:

  • use it with a keyboard, including the Prompt Cycle steps (use the arrow keys);
  • use it with a screen reader;
  • zoom to 400% without the text spilling off the screen;
  • turn off animation through your device's "reduce motion" setting.

Tell us about a problem

If something doesn't work for you, or you need information in another format such as easy read or large print, email info@nursingcitizendevelopment.com. We'll reply within 10 working days.

Testing

This statement was prepared on 20 November 2026 from our own checks. We have not yet had an independent accessibility audit.
