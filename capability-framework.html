How our resources map to the NHS AI capability framework

Mapped 20 November 2026 by Nursing Citizen Development

NHS England's Artificial Intelligence and Digital Healthcare Technologies Capability Framework (developed for Health Education England by the University of Manchester) sets out what health and care staff need to know about AI and digital technology. It has 6 domains, 11 sub-domains and 195 capability statements at 4 levels, from 1 (foundation) to 4 (expert).

Our free AI Nursing Toolkit and Nursing Copilot Handbook mainly support levels 1 and 2, for the framework's Users and Embedders: the nurses who use AI in daily work and help their teams adopt it safely. Some material reaches level 3 and supports Drivers and Creators.

How to read this

  • Strong: dedicated pages or chapters that cover the domain at the levels shown.
  • Partial: some content, but not all of the domain's capability statements.
  • Gap: not covered yet. Remote consultation and monitoring, and robotics, are on our list for future work.

This mapping is by domain and sub-domain, not by each of the 195 capability statements. It is our own mapping and does not mean NHS England has reviewed or endorsed our resources. The framework was last reviewed in 2023, so use it alongside current NHS guidance.
