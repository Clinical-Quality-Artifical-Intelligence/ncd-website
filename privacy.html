Who is responsible for your information

Nursing Citizen Development CIC is the controller of the personal information described here. We are a community interest company registered in England and Wales, company no. 17324367. Registered office: Flat 2 Fenton House, Hickman Street, Newcastle, ST5 2AR. Contact: info@nursingcitizendevelopment.com.

What we collect and why

When you visit this website. Our host, GitHub Pages, records technical information such as your IP address in its server logs to keep the site secure and running. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in running a secure website. If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics also collects information about your visit (see Cookies). Our lawful basis for analytics is your consent.

When you email us. We use your message and email address to reply. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in answering enquiries.

When you use our feedback form. Microsoft Forms processes your answers for us. Only give personal information if you want us to contact you. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in improving our resources, or your consent where the form asks for it.

Emails about our work. If you ask to hear from us, we only email you with your consent, which you can withdraw at any time by replying to any email.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Cookies

A cookie is a small file stored by your browser. We use only the cookies below.

_ga, _ga_NNVRRR8ZN8Analytics (optional)Google Analytics: counts visits and shows which pages are read, roughly where visitors are (country or city, not your exact location), what device they use, which website sent them, and which of our free resources they open.Up to 2 years

We only set analytics cookies if you click "Accept analytics cookies". If you reject them, or make no choice, Google Analytics does not load at all. Google Signals and advertising features are switched off, and IP addresses are anonymised. Analytics data is kept for 14 months.

. If you withdraw consent, we delete the analytics cookies from your browser.

Our fonts are stored on this website, so no information is sent to font providers. Pages on other sites we link to, such as our toolkit at practicedev.cloud, have their own privacy notices.

Who we share it with

  • GitHub: hosting this website.
  • Google: Google Analytics, only if you accept analytics cookies.
  • Microsoft: email and Microsoft Forms.

These providers process information for us under contract. We may disclose information if the law requires us to.

Transfers outside the UK

GitHub, Google and Microsoft may process information in the United States and other countries. Where they do, the transfer is protected by the UK's adequacy regulations for the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or by the UK International Data Transfer Addendum to standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

  • Google Analytics data: 14 months.
  • Emails and feedback: up to two years after the matter is closed.
  • Email preferences: until you ask us to stop.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your information, ask us to correct or delete it, object to or restrict how we use it, ask for it in a portable format, and withdraw consent at any time. Email info@nursingcitizendevelopment.com and we will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have used your information, please tell us first. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office: ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint, or call 0303 123 1113.

Changes

We will update this notice if how we use information changes, and show the date at the top.
