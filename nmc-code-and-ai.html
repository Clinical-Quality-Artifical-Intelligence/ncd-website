UK Nursing Regulation & Safe AI

The NMC Code and AI in nursing: accountability, confidentiality and revalidation

Published 28 November 2026 by Lincoln Gombedza, Founder of Nursing Citizen Development CIC · Educational resource (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) — independent of the NMC and not formal regulatory or legal advice.

How does the NMC Code apply to artificial intelligence? The NMC Code does not prohibit nurses, midwives, or nursing associates from using AI tools — but every professional duty in the Code still applies in full. You remain personally accountable for patient confidentiality, clinical accuracy, evidence-based practice, and every record or reflection published in your name.

Copilot does not hold an NMC PIN. ChatGPT does not pay registration fees. When AI enters clinical admin, teaching, or revalidation, the NMC Code's four themes give nurses a clear, practical compass.

Mapping AI use to the four themes of the NMC Code

NMC Code theme What it means when using AI Practical nursing guardrail
1. Prioritise people Respect dignity, informed consent, accessibility, and privacy at every step. Never paste patient-identifiable data into unapproved AI tools; check outputs for cultural, racial, or disability bias before using them in care education.
2. Practise effectively Base care and teaching on verified best evidence and keep clear, accurate records. Cross-check every AI-generated clinical statement, dose, or citation against BNF, NICE, or Trust policy — AI confidently invents references.
3. Preserve safety Recognise and work within limits of competence; escalate digital safety hazards. Never delegate triage, prescribing, safeguarding, or clinical decisions to a chatbot; report unsafe AI outputs through local Trust incident routes.
4. Promote professionalism and trust Be honest, transparent, and authentic in records, research, and revalidation. Use AI to coach and structure your thinking — never to fabricate CPD logs, coursework, or NMC reflective accounts.

Using AI safely for NMC revalidation and CPD

Revalidation is about genuine professional learning and reflection. Safe, NMC-aligned ways to use AI for CPD and revalidation include:

  • Reflective coaching (not ghostwriting): Ask AI to walk you through Gibbs or Driscoll reflective questions one by one while you write your own authentic, anonymised account.
  • Mapping CPD to the Code: Ask AI to help you spot which NMC Code themes connect to a general clinical learning topic (without sharing identifiable cases).
  • Preparing for reflective discussions: Use AI to generate practice discussion prompts ahead of your confirmation or appraisal meeting.

Frequently asked questions

Does the NMC Code ban ChatGPT or Microsoft Copilot?

No — the Code sets professional principles rather than naming specific software. However, you must only use AI tools permitted by your NHS Trust, university, or employer, and never compromise patient confidentiality or clinical safety.

Is removing a patient's name enough to make a prompt safe?

No. Combining a rare condition, approximate age, date, and ward can still identify a patient (jigsaw identification). Never enter clinical case details into unapproved public AI tools — use general, non-identifiable scenarios instead.

Will Nursing Citizen Development update its resources for the new NMC Code?

Yes. Both The Nursing Copilot Handbook and the living NCD AI Nursing Toolkit are committed to free updates as the NMC Code consultation and revised Code are published.
